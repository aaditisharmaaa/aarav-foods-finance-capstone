Aarav Foods | Financial Analysis & Expansion Feasibility Capstone

A Business Analytics capstone project focused on evaluating Aarav Foods’ financial performance, benchmarking it against industry peers, and assessing the feasibility of a proposed business expansion.

⸻

1. Project Overview

This project analyses the financial position and business performance of Aarav Foods through peer benchmarking, financial ratio analysis and expansion feasibility assessment.

The objective is to evaluate the company’s profitability, capital efficiency, working-capital management and financial structure, while identifying potential risks and opportunities associated with its proposed expansion.

The project combines Excel-based financial analysis with interactive Power BI visualisation to translate financial data into actionable business insights and support management decision-making.

2. Business Problem

Aarav Foods needs to evaluate its financial performance relative to competing companies and determine whether its financial position can support the proposed expansion.

The analysis focuses on answering the following questions:

* How does Aarav Foods perform compared with its industry peers?
* Is the company generating sustainable operating profitability?
* How efficiently is capital being utilised?
* Is working capital being managed effectively?
* What financial risks could affect the proposed expansion?
* What should management monitor before and during implementation?

3. Project Objectives

1. Conduct financial benchmarking across five companies.
2. Evaluate profitability, liquidity-related working-capital efficiency and capital utilisation.
3. Compare ROE and ROCE to understand differences in shareholder returns and capital efficiency.
4. Assess the quality of reported profits by examining non-operating income.
5. Evaluate the financial implications and feasibility of the proposed expansion.
6. Present findings through an interactive Power BI dashboard.
7. Develop a data-backed recommendation for management.

5. Financial Metrics Analysed

The project uses key financial indicators to assess company performance.

Metric	What it measures
Operating Profit Margin	Profitability generated from core operations
Net Profit Margin	Profit remaining after expenses and taxes
ROCE	Efficiency of capital employed in generating returns
ROE	Returns generated on shareholders’ equity
Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC)	Efficiency of working-capital conversion
Non-operating Share of PBT	Contribution of non-operating income to profit before tax

These metrics provide a combined view of profitability, capital efficiency and earnings quality.

6. Key Financial Insights

One of the important findings from the peer comparison concerns Ruchi Dairy.

* ROE: 17.08%
* ROCE: 6.00%
* Non-operating share of PBT: 78.0%

Although Ruchi Dairy reports a relatively high ROE, its significantly lower ROCE and substantial dependence on non-operating income suggest that shareholder returns should not be interpreted in isolation.

This highlights the importance of examining the source and sustainability of profits rather than relying on a single financial ratio.

The analysis also uses peer benchmarking to understand Aarav Foods’ relative financial position and identify areas requiring management attention.

7. Power BI Dashboard

The Power BI report contains two pages designed to make financial performance easier to explore and compare.

Page 1: Financial Performance Overview

Includes:

* Operating Profit Margin KPI card
* ROCE KPI card
* ROE KPI card
* Cash Conversion Cycle KPI card
* Company slicer for interactive company selection

Page 2: Peer Comparison & Profitability Analysis

Includes:

* Net Profit Margin comparison
* ROE versus ROCE comparison
* Non-operating share of profit comparison

The dashboard helps users move from individual financial indicators to broader peer-level insights.

It also provides a monitoring framework using operating margin, CCC, ROCE and ROE to support periodic management reviews during the proposed expansion.

8. Business Recommendation & Execution

The project connects financial benchmarking with the proposed expansion decision.

Rather than evaluating expansion purely through a growth perspective, the analysis considers financial sustainability, operating profitability, capital efficiency and working-capital requirements.

The dashboard provides measurable indicators that management can revisit at different stages of implementation.

These include:

* Operating margin to monitor core business profitability.
* CCC to monitor working-capital efficiency.
* ROCE to evaluate returns on invested capital.
* ROE to track shareholder returns.

This approach supports a more structured and measurable decision-making process.
